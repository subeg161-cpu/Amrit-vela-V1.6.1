🪷 Pyari Bani
Add as many Banis as you like. Each keeps its own quantity, lifetime total, streak and achievements under a permanent internal ID, so renaming never detaches history. Archiving hides a Bani from Home but keeps everything it has ever recorded.
Active Banis
Archived — history kept
How the points work
Pyari Bani is a weighted category in your daily score, like Panj Granthi. Its total weight is set under Scoring & Analytics → Scoring Weights (ਪਿਆਰੀ ਬਾਣੀ, default 12). Each Bani's own point value is its share of that weight. The first completion in a day earns 100% of a Bani's points; each extra completion that day earns another 25%. The category can never earn more than its weight. It counts toward the weighted and Discipline scores and XP — not toward the spiritual score, streaks or Perfect Day. A day keeps the point values and Banis it was logged under, so editing points affects new activity, not history.
